🌾 Bangla Farm Navigator

Bangla Farm Navigator is a simple and user-friendly mobile app designed for Bangladeshi farmers. The app provides crop recommendations, weather information, and agricultural guidelines based on the farmer’s location.

✨ Features: 📍 Location-based Suggestions – Agricultural advice based on geographic region 🌱 Crop Recommendation – Best crops for your specific area ☀️ Weather Information – Weather updates for farming decisions 📖 Agricultural Guidelines – Helpful tips and guides for farmers
